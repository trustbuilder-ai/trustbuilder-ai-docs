## Primary Customer Segments

### Segment 1: Financial Services Enterprises

#### Key Decision Makers

**Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)**
- **Responsibilities**: Overall AI security strategy, risk management
- **Pain Points**: DORA compliance, real-time fraud detection AI risks
- **Budget Authority**: $5-50M annual security budget
- **Success Metrics**: Zero critical incidents, regulatory compliance
- **Buying Triggers**: Regulatory deadlines, board mandates, incidents

**AI Governance Officer**
- **Responsibilities**: AI ethics, compliance, risk frameworks
- **Pain Points**: Lack of standardized testing methodologies
- **Budget Authority**: $1-10M for AI governance tools
- **Success Metrics**: Successful audits, no regulatory fines
- **Buying Triggers**: New AI deployments, audit findings

**Head of Model Risk Management**
- **Responsibilities**: Model validation, risk assessment
- **Pain Points**: Extending traditional model risk to GenAI
- **Budget Authority**: $2-20M for validation tools
- **Success Metrics**: Model performance, risk metrics
- **Buying Triggers**: New model deployments, regulatory changes

#### Industry-Specific Requirements
- **Regulations**: DORA, EU AI Act, Basel III, MiFID II
- **Use Cases**: Credit scoring, fraud detection, trading algorithms, customer service chatbots
- **Risk Tolerance**: Very low - single incident can trigger regulatory action
- **Testing Frequency**: Continuous for production models
- **Compliance Needs**: Detailed audit trails, model documentation

### Segment 2: Healthcare Organizations

#### Key Decision Makers

**Chief Medical Information Officer (CMIO)**
- **Responsibilities**: Clinical AI deployment, patient safety
- **Pain Points**: Ensuring AI doesn't compromise patient care
- **Budget Authority**: $2-15M for clinical AI tools
- **Success Metrics**: Patient outcomes, clinical efficiency
- **Buying Triggers**: New diagnostic AI tools, FDA requirements

**Compliance Director**
- **Responsibilities**: HIPAA, GDPR, FDA compliance
- **Pain Points**: AI transparency requirements, data privacy
- **Budget Authority**: $1-5M for compliance tools
- **Success Metrics**: Zero violations, successful audits
- **Buying Triggers**: Regulatory updates, audit preparation

**VP of Digital Health Innovation**
- **Responsibilities**: AI strategy, digital transformation
- **Pain Points**: Balancing innovation with safety
- **Budget Authority**: $5-30M for digital initiatives
- **Success Metrics**: AI adoption rate, ROI
- **Buying Triggers**: Competitive pressure, strategic initiatives

#### Industry-Specific Requirements
- **Regulations**: HIPAA, GDPR, FDA AI/ML guidance, EU MDR
- **Use Cases**: Diagnostic AI, treatment recommendations, patient triage, administrative automation
- **Risk Tolerance**: Extremely low - patient safety paramount
- **Testing Frequency**: Pre-deployment intensive, ongoing monitoring
- **Compliance Needs**: Clinical validation, bias testing, explainability

---

### Segment 3: Critical Infrastructure Operators

#### Key Decision Makers

**Chief Security Officer (CSO)**
- **Responsibilities**: Physical and cyber security
- **Pain Points**: AI in OT environments, nation-state threats
- **Budget Authority**: $10-100M security budget
- **Success Metrics**: System uptime, threat prevention
- **Buying Triggers**: Security incidents, government mandates

**Director of Operational Technology**
- **Responsibilities**: Industrial control systems, SCADA
- **Pain Points**: AI integration with legacy systems
- **Budget Authority**: $5-50M for OT modernization
- **Success Metrics**: Operational efficiency, safety metrics
- **Buying Triggers**: Modernization projects, incidents

**Risk & Compliance Manager**
- **Responsibilities**: NIS2 compliance, risk assessment
- **Pain Points**: Executive liability under NIS2
- **Budget Authority**: $2-10M for compliance tools
- **Success Metrics**: Compliance scores, risk reduction
- **Buying Triggers**: NIS2 deadlines, board pressure

#### Industry-Specific Requirements
- **Regulations**: NIS2 Directive, sector-specific (energy, transport, water)
- **Use Cases**: Predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, resource optimization
- **Risk Tolerance**: Zero tolerance for operational disruption
- **Testing Frequency**: Extensive pre-production, careful production rollout
- **Compliance Needs**: Resilience testing, incident response capabilities

---

### Segment 4: Technology Companies

#### Key Decision Makers

**VP of AI/ML Engineering**
- **Responsibilities**: AI product development, deployment
- **Pain Points**: Shipping secure AI features quickly
- **Budget Authority**: $5-50M for AI infrastructure
- **Success Metrics**: Feature velocity, model performance
- **Buying Triggers**: Product launches, customer requirements

**Chief Trust Officer**
- **Responsibilities**: Customer trust, brand protection
- **Pain Points**: Public AI failures, trust erosion
- **Budget Authority**: $2-20M for trust initiatives
- **Success Metrics**: Trust scores, brand sentiment
- **Buying Triggers**: Competitor incidents, customer concerns

**Product Security Lead**
- **Responsibilities**: Security in SDLC, vulnerability management
- **Pain Points**: AI-specific security gaps
- **Budget Authority**: $1-10M for security tools
- **Success Metrics**: Vulnerability metrics, incident prevention
- **Buying Triggers**: Security reviews, customer audits

#### Industry-Specific Requirements
- **Regulations**: EU AI Act (if serving EU), SOC 2, ISO 27001
- **Use Cases**: Customer-facing AI features, internal productivity tools
- **Risk Tolerance**: Moderate - balance innovation with safety
- **Testing Frequency**: CI/CD integration, continuous
- **Compliance Needs**: Customer audit support, security certifications

## Buyer Journey Mapping

### Awareness Stage
**Triggers:**
- Regulatory announcement/deadline
- Competitor AI incident
- Board inquiry about AI risks
- Failed audit

**Information Seeking:**
- Industry reports on AI security
- Peer recommendations
- Analyst briefings (Gartner, Forrester)
- Conference presentations

### Consideration Stage
**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Regulatory coverage
- Testing methodology
- Expertise/credibility
- Integration complexity
- Pricing model

**Proof Points Needed:**
- Customer case studies
- Security certifications
- Analyst recognition
- ROI data

### Decision Stage
**Stakeholders Involved:**
- Technical: CISO, Engineering
- Business: CFO, Risk Committee
- Legal: General Counsel, Compliance

**Final Concerns:**
- Vendor stability
- Support quality
- Scalability
- Contract terms

### Implementation Stage
**Success Factors:**
- Smooth onboarding
- Quick time to value
- Clear documentation
- Responsive support

## Objection Handling by Segment

### Common Objections & Responses

**"We have internal red teams"**
- Response: "TrustBuilder augments your team with 10,000+ external perspectives, finding novel attacks your team hasn't considered."

**"We use automated scanning tools"**
- Response: "Automation catches known patterns. Human creativity discovers the unknown vulnerabilities that cause breaches."

**"It's too expensive"**
- Response: "One prevented incident pays for years of TrustBuilder. Plus, it's 60% less than consultant-led testing."

**"We're concerned about IP exposure"**
- Response: "Models are tested anonymously with strict NDAs. We're SOC 2 certified with zero breach history."
