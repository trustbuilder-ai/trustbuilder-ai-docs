## Objection Handling Scripts

### "We already have security tools"
**Response**: "Your current tools were built for traditional software, not AI. TrustBuilder specifically tests prompt injection, model manipulation, and MCP vulnerabilities that generic scanners miss. We complement your existing stack with AI-specific validation."

### "This could expose our IP"
**Response**: "Your models are tested anonymously under strict NDAs. We're SOC 2 certified with bank-level security. Plus, our researchers never see your training data or model weights – only the API interface."

### "Crowdsourcing sounds risky"
**Response**: "Our researchers are vetted professionals, not random hackers. Every tester signs NDAs and operates within strict ethical guidelines. The diversity of perspectives actually reduces risk by finding vulnerabilities a single team would miss."

### "It's too expensive"
**Response**: "At 60% less than consulting firms and preventing just one incident that could cost millions, TrustBuilder pays for itself. Plus, our usage-based pricing means you only pay for the validation you need."

### "We don't have time for this"
**Response**: "Integration takes less than a day. Testing runs continuously in the background. You get actionable reports without disrupting development. The time you save from prevented incidents far exceeds the setup investment."
